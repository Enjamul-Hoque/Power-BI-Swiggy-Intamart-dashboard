# Swiggy-Instamart-dashboard


## Business Problem

The business wanted a single dashboard to track sales, orders, ratings, shop performance, and product categories. By analyzing this data, management can find top-performing products and stores, understand customer preferences, and make better business decisions to grow revenue.s
## Tools Used

* Power BI
* Power Query
* Excel

## DAX Measures Used

* Total_Order = COUNTROWS('Grocery Shop')
* Total Sales = SUM('Grocery Shop'[Sales]) 
* Ave_Sales = AVERAGE('Grocery Shop'[Sales])
* Avg_Rating = AVERAGE('Grocery Shop'[Rating])


## KPIs

*  Total_Order = 8.5K
* Total Sales = 1.20M
* Ave_Sales = 141
* Avg_Rating = 3.92


## Key Insights

* Fruits & Vegetables generated the highest sales (178.15K).
* Snack Foods ranked second (175.43K).
* Household products also contributed significantly (135.98K).


## Recommendations

* Maintain sufficient inventory for top-selling categories.
* Create promotional bundles for Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods.
