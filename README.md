# Swiggy-Instamart-dashboard


## Business Problem

The business wanted a single dashboard to track sales, orders, ratings, shop performance, and product categories. By analyzing this data, management can find top-performing products and stores, understand customer preferences, and make better business decisions to grow revenue.s
## Tools Used

* Power BI
* Power Query
* Excel

## DAX Measures Used

* Total_Order = COUNTROWS('Grocery Shop')
* Total Sales = SUM('Grocery Shop'[Sales]) 
* Ave_Sales = AVERAGE('Grocery Shop'[Sales])
* Avg_Rating = AVERAGE('Grocery Shop'[Rating])


## KPIs

*  Total_Order = 8.5K
* Total Sales = 1.20M
* Ave_Sales = 141
* Avg_Rating = 3.92


## Key Insights

* Samosa recorded the highest quantity sold (2,427 units).
* Butter Chicken was the second most ordered item (2,044 units).
* Chole Bhature and Masala Dosa also showed strong customer demand.
* These items are the main contributors to overall order volume.

## Recommendations

* Increase promotions for Samosa and Butter Chicken.
* Ensure sufficient inventory for high-demand items.
* Create combo offers using top-selling foods.
